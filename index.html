Questions

Things people ask before booking

How much do I pay to confirm a booking?

A 25% deposit is required to confirm your booking. The balance is due 21 days before departure.

Can I change my dates after booking?

Yes. Date and hotel changes are free up to 21 days before departure, subject to availability. Airline fare differences, if any, are passed on at cost.

Do you help with visas?

We guide you through documents and submit applications for Dubai, Thailand, Singapore and Schengen countries. Final approval is decided by the embassy.

What if something goes wrong during my trip?

Call our 24/7 helpline. Your advisor and a local partner will rebook, reroute or arrange alternatives as quickly as possible.

Can you plan a trip around my budget?

Yes. Share a budget range and we will suggest the best-value options, and tell you honestly where spending more is worth it.
